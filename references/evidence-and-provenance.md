# Evidence and provenance

Use this reference when the interface contains claims, metrics, customer
material, third-party marks, research findings or external imagery.

## Record for every public-facing item

- exact claim or asset description;
- source/evidence ID and date;
- fact vs inference vs proposal;
- owner who can approve or withdraw it;
- rights/privacy status;
- where it appears in the UI;
- fallback if the evidence is withdrawn.

Claims must not be strengthened by visual treatment. A large number, client
logo, testimonial, “trusted by” wall or performance badge needs the same source
standard as body copy. If evidence is unavailable, remove the claim or mark it
`待确认`; never fill the gap with plausible-looking UI.
